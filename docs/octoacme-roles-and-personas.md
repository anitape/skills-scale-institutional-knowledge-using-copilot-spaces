# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate readiness, scheduling, and communication for production releases. They help ensure releases happen safely and with clear visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate release timelines and deployment windows
- Confirm release readiness across engineering, QA, and stakeholders
- Track release notes, rollback plans, and post-deploy checks
- Communicate release status, risks, and changes to stakeholders

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and avoid surprises
- Improve visibility into release readiness
- Ensure production changes are coordinated and documented

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with PM, QA, and Developers
- Deployment announcements and stakeholder updates
- Coordination with support and operations during release windows

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to confirm scope and timing for release.
- Works with Project Managers to track release milestones and dependencies.
- Coordinates with Developers to verify fixes, merges, and deployment requirements.
- Aligns with QA/Testing to confirm sign-off and smoke test results.
- Updates Stakeholders on expected impact, timing, and rollback readiness.

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the validation strategy and help ensure features meet quality expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans, coverage goals, and acceptance validation approach
- Coordinate QA execution, including regression and smoke testing
- Track defects, test results, and release blockers
- Partner with Developers to improve testability and fix verification

### Goals
- Catch issues before release
- Improve confidence in delivery quality
- Make acceptance criteria measurable and testable

### Typical Communication
- Test strategy and defect triage meetings
- QA status updates in standups and release reviews
- Feedback on acceptance criteria and test gaps

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to validate acceptance criteria and expected behavior.
- Works with Project Managers to surface quality risks and testing dependencies.
- Partners with Developers on bug fixes, reproduction steps, and automation opportunities.
- Supports Release Managers with QA sign-off, smoke tests, and go/no-go decisions.
- Communicates quality status to Stakeholders when needed.

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, design decisions, and dependency management across the project.

### Responsibilities
- Review architecture and implementation approaches
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Support design decisions and trade-off discussions
- Help unblock complex technical work and integration issues

### Goals
- Keep solutions maintainable and scalable
- Reduce technical uncertainty and rework
- Improve alignment between architecture and delivery needs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical planning sessions
- Risk and dependency discussions with PM and Developers
- Architecture notes or decision records when needed

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on technical feasibility and trade-offs.
- Works with Developers to clarify implementation approach and resolve blockers.
- Supports QA/Testing by clarifying expected behavior and integration points.
- Helps Release Managers assess technical readiness and deployment risk.
- May brief Stakeholders on major technical constraints or decisions.

---

## Stakeholder Sponsors

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Sponsors provide executive or business sponsorship for the project and help align priorities, funding, and approvals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm business priority and expected outcomes
- Support escalation decisions and trade-offs
- Provide approvals when scope, timing, or risk changes
- Help remove organizational blockers when needed

### Goals
- Ensure the project stays aligned to business value
- Support timely decisions and escalation resolution
- Maintain sponsorship visibility throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and executive updates
- Escalation meetings for risks or decisions
- Approval checkpoints at major project gates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to confirm goals, value, and priority.
- Aligns with Project Managers on schedule, risk, and escalation needs.
- Receives release and status updates from Release Managers.
- Provides direction when Developers, QA, or delivery leads need scope or priority decisions.

---

## Operations / Support Leads

### Role Summary
Operations and Support Leads prepare the team for handoff, production support, and incident response after release.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support readiness and handoff materials
- Confirm operational monitoring, alerts, and runbooks are in place
- Support incident triage and post-release verification when needed
- Capture operational feedback and recurring issues for improvement

### Goals
- Ensure a smooth transition to support and operations
- Reduce production support risk
- Improve readiness for incidents and ongoing maintenance

### Typical Communication
- Handoff and readiness reviews before release
- Incident or support triage communication after release
- Operational feedback loops with delivery and product teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Release Managers on deployment readiness and post-release support planning.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on handoff timing, dependencies, and escalation.
- Collaborates with Developers to document fixes, known issues, and operational requirements.
- Supports QA/Testing with production-like verification when needed.
- Shares support trends and risks with Product Managers and Stakeholders.
